The Falling Grace App

Pentatonic and Rhythmic Permutations

The Falling Grace App, based on Steve Swallow's composition Falling Grace, is an interactive jazz improvisation tool inspired by Mick Goodrick's 24 rhythmic permutations. It generates melodic variations over a fixed 24-measure harmonic progression using carefully selected pentatonic scales.

The application draws from three pentatonic types:

  • Major pentatonic: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6
  • Minor pentatonic: 1, ♭3, 4, 5, ♭7
  • Dominant 7♯9 pentatonic: 1, ♯2, 3, 5, ♭7 (C, D♯, E, G, B♭)

Each chord has a curated selection of compatible pentatonics. The generator randomly selects one permitted five-note collection per measure, preserving its pitch classes rather than combining notes from multiple scales.

All 24 of Goodrick's rhythmic patterns appear exactly once per exercise in randomized order. Melodic generation follows defined constraints governing register, interval size, rhythmic ties, and harmonic compatibility.

Four melodic modes, Angular, Smooth, Triadic, and Quartal, provide different approaches to organizing the selected pitches.

Built with JavaScript, ABCJS notation, and sample-based audio playback, Falling Grace provides synchronized notation and playback, adjustable tempo and range, piano and Rhodes sounds, and downloadable MIDI, WAV, and PDF files.

The result is controlled musical randomness, producing a virtually unlimited variety of melodic exercises within clearly defined harmonic, rhythmic, and intervallic boundaries.

This application is not intended to replace the combination of pencil, manuscript paper, and your ears that Mick Goodrick advocated. Rather, it offers a complementary way to hear the musical possibilities created by these constraints, develop fluency with the resulting patterns under your fingers, and strengthen your sight-reading skills through continually changing melodic material.
